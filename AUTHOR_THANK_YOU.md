# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Rui Ai, Yuqi Pan, David Simchi-Levi, Milind Tambe, and Haifeng Xu
for developing and sharing the higher-order LLM aggregation perspective. The
OW and ISP constructions, exact ordering arguments, and ensemble analysis make
the question of when voting is insufficient unusually concrete.

This repository is an independent clean-room audit. It preserves the paper
authors and their claims, records what can be checked from public equations and
tables, and leaves the real-data route blocked rather than replacing the
authors’ inference records with an unrelated experiment.
